Frequently Asked Questions

What is the "Google Guaranteed" badge? +

To get the badge, Google performs license and background checks. Once approved, Google backs the jobs you book through LSA, giving peace of mind to your customers.

How does paying per lead work? +

You set a budget, and Google only charges you when a customer calls you, sends a message, or books directly through the ad. If a spammer calls, we dispute the lead for a refund.
