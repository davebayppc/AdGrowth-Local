Política de Privacidad

Last updated: April 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to AdGrowth Local's Privacy Policy. This document explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our website or contact us for our services.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website, request a quote, or contact us, we understand the importance of your privacy. We may collect the following basic information:

  • Personal Details: Your name
  • Contact Information: Your email address and phone number

3. How We Use Your Information

We collect and use your data strictly for legitimate, required business purposes. Specifically, we use the information mentioned above solely to:

  • Provide you with accurate service estimates, audits, and custom proposals.
  • Communicate with you regarding our services, consultations, and potential campaigns.

4. We Do Not Sell Your Data

We respect your privacy. AdGrowth Local does not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes. Any information you provide is kept strictly confidential and used solely to assist you with our direct services.

5. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies, Google Tag Manager (GTM), and tracking pixels to measure campaign performance, understand site usage, and show relevant ads.

6. Third-Party Sharing

We share data with our trusted service providers, such as Google Ads and CallRail, strictly for service facilitation and tracking. We do not share data for third-party independent marketing.

7. Opt-Out Instructions

You have control over your data. To opt-out of personalized advertising, you can visit Google's Ad Settings or access the Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) opt-out page.
