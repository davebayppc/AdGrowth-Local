About AdGrowth Local

We exist to bring simple, transparent, and profitable lead generation to local service businesses.

Hi, I’m David M. Bayas, an independent Paid Media Specialist.

After years in the digital marketing industry, I saw a frustrating pattern: traditional agencies charging premium retainers just to cover corporate overhead and account managers. I left that world to build a smarter, more cost-effective alternative for local small businesses.

By operating as an independent freelancer, I eliminate the agency bloat. You pay for high-level expertise and execution, not expensive office space. This keeps your costs grounded, leaving you with better cash flow and more budget to put directly into your campaigns.


Dedicated Attention & Freelance Agility

Unlike agencies that overload their staff with accounts, I intentionally maintain a selective, limited client roster. This ensures that every business I partner with receives the dedicated time, meticulous attention, and high-quality management it deserves.

My day-to-day focus is strictly on driving immediate calls and bookings through Google Ads, Local Service Ads (LSA), Microsoft Ads, and Meta Ads. When a campaign requires a high-converting landing page or compelling visuals, I partner with a trusted network of independent web developers and ad creators. You get the comprehensive capabilities of an agency, with the care and agility of dedicated freelancers.


Why Work With Us?

  • Direct Accountability: You work directly with me—the specialist actively building and optimizing your ad spend.
  • Adaptable Tracking: Reliable data is everything. I configure conversion tracking to work seamlessly with the tools your business already uses.
  • Bilingual Campaigns: As a fluent English and Spanish speaker, I build campaigns that connect with a wider local audience.
  • Honest Reporting: No overnight guarantees or confusing vanity metrics. We focus on steady, sustainable growth and explain your results.

My goal is simply to be the reliable partner that helps you build a consistent lead-generation engine.
